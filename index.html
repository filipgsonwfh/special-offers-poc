Pick a variant

Each variant opens as a working page, on desktop or in a phone frame. Partner packages are one offer per page, co-branded with the partner. Listing cards lead into the campaign pages, breadcrumbs lead back, and every Search trip or Book button lands on a booking stub that shows what would be handed to the booking flow. On a phone, every page switches to its mobile layout by itself.

Listing pages

Listing with filters One list for all offers. Filter chips replace the separate Day trips page; a featured campaign slot sits on top.
Guided deal finder Three questions instead of a filter bar. Each answer rewrites the shortlist and promotes the best match.

Campaign pages

Cruise hub Several products on one page. Product tiles in the booking panel; themed sailings as dated chips that deep-link into booking.
Sailing calendar The same cruises as a calendar. Filter by product, pick a date, book from the side panel.
Sale with deal selector A sale with four offer codes. The selected deal’s code is applied automatically.
Editorial, booking first The sale as a long read with the booking panel in the hero and one chapter per deal.
Editorial with deal bar The long read with a sticky deal bar instead; booking panel at the end.

Partner packages

Partner package, one offer per page Ferry, hotel, tickets and dinner at one price, with the partner (LEGOLAND®, Liseberg) as the headline and Stena Line and Sembo side by side as sellers. Opens on the LEGOLAND® package; the other packages are linked from the bottom of the page.
Same template, Liseberg as partner Two days at Liseberg, two nights at Gothia Towers across the street and a spa package, from Frederikshavn.
