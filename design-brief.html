The problem today

A special-offer page is bound to one product code and one Find-your-trip widget. Theme cruises therefore need a page per product (24h, 36h), and a sale with several products needs two or three landing pages. The listing is a flat list with Day trips as a separate page.

The proposal

  • A Campaign page holds any number of bookable products. The booking panel gets a product selector; the selected product's code goes to booking.
  • Fixed-date sailings are departures that deep-link into booking with product + dates (as in the PL proposal).
  • Modules (what's included, prices, terms) can vary per product. Listing merges Day trips as a filter.

Decisions I made for you

  • Product selector as radio tiles for up to 4 products, a dropdown beyond that.
  • Both booking paths: widget for open-date offers, deep links for fixed dates.
  • Examples: PL cruises to Sweden (1b) and a UK Winter Sale (1c). Codes marked * and 36h/regular prices are placeholders.
  • Desktop first, one mobile view (1d). Stena Sans is shown with the Figtree stand-in.
